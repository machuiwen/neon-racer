# Neon Racer

A neon synthwave racing game that runs in the browser. Race three computer cars around a glowing track at sunset.

**Play it here: https://machuiwen.github.io/neon-racer/**

Works on computers, iPads, and phones, in landscape or portrait.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Touch screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Left / Right arrows, or A / D | Big arrow buttons (bottom left) |
| Gas | Up arrow or W | GO button (or turn on **Auto gas** on the start screen) |
| Brake / reverse | Down arrow or S | BRAKE button |
| Boost | Space | BOOST button |
| Pause | P or Esc | Pause button (top right) |
| Sound on/off | M | Speaker button (top right) |

## Tips

- Grab the blue diamonds to fill your boost bar.
- Slide around corners to fill your boost bar too.
- Collect the gold stars for fun.
- **Easy** level helps you steer and keeps you off the walls.
- Your best lap time is saved on your device.

## Notes

- On iPhone and iPad, the sound follows the silent switch. If you hear nothing, flip the switch to ring and check the speaker button.
- Needs a recent browser (Safari 16.4 or newer on iPad).
- Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) 0.160.0 from a CDN. All sound is made live with Web Audio, no audio files. No build step: open `index.html` from any static web server.
